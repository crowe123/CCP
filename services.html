What We Do

Our Cleanup Services

Comprehensive, compassionate cleanup services for every situation. All services include free estimates and are 100% confidential.

Hoarding Cleanup

Our core service addresses hoarding situations at every level. We work with homeowners, families, and social service agencies to restore homes to safe, livable conditions. The process begins with a confidential assessment where we evaluate the scope of work, identify any hazards (mold, biohazard, structural concerns), and develop a custom cleanup plan.

Our team is trained in the psychology of hoarding and approaches every job with empathy and patience. We never rush the process or make the homeowner feel judged. Items are sorted collaboratively — the homeowner decides what stays, what gets donated, and what gets discarded. For clients who are unable to be present, we work with their designated representative to make decisions about belongings.

Houston's climate makes professional hoarding cleanup especially important. Mold, pests, and moisture damage can develop rapidly in our subtropical environment, turning a cluttered home into a health hazard. Our team uses commercial-grade HEPA filtration, industrial sanitizers, and professional deodorization equipment to ensure the home is thoroughly cleaned and safe after item removal.

Estate Cleanouts

When a loved one passes away or moves to assisted living, the family is often left with a home full of decades of accumulated belongings. Our estate cleanout service handles the entire process — sorting through personal items, identifying valuables, coordinating donations, and removing everything else. We treat inherited property with the same respect and care we bring to every job.

Estate cleanouts in Houston often involve homes that have been closed up for weeks or months. In our climate, this means potential mold growth, pest infiltration, and odor issues that compound the cleanup challenge. We address all of these issues as part of our comprehensive service.

Deep Cleaning & Sanitization

After items are removed from a hoarded home, the surfaces underneath often need intensive cleaning. Our deep cleaning service includes scrubbing floors, walls, cabinets, countertops, and fixtures. We use hospital-grade disinfectants to eliminate bacteria, mold spores, and allergens. HEPA vacuuming removes fine particulates from carpets and upholstery.

For homes with severe contamination, we may recommend carpet removal and replacement, wall repainting, or other restoration services. We coordinate with trusted Houston contractors for any repairs beyond our scope.

Biohazard Cleanup

Some hoarding situations involve biohazardous materials — human or animal waste, bodily fluids, decomposing food, or medical waste. Our team is trained in OSHA bloodborne pathogen standards and uses appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) and disposal methods for all biohazard situations.

Biohazard materials are disposed of according to Texas Department of State Health Services (DSHS) regulations. We document the cleanup process and can provide reports for insurance or legal purposes when needed.

Junk Removal & Hauling

We handle all aspects of item removal and disposal. This includes furniture, appliances, electronics, clothing, paper, and general household items. We sort items for recycling, donation, and disposal to minimize landfill waste. Large items and heavy loads are no problem — our crews have the equipment and vehicles to handle any volume.

All disposal is handled responsibly and in compliance with City of Houston and Harris County waste regulations. We can coordinate with specialized recyclers for electronics, paint, chemicals, and other items that require special handling.

Odor Removal & Air Quality Restoration

Hoarded homes often have severe odor issues from mold, mildew, decomposing materials, pet waste, and general stagnation. Our odor remediation service uses thermal fogging, ozone treatment, hydroxyl generators, and enzyme-based cleaners to neutralize odors at the molecular level — not just mask them.

We also address the source of odors by removing contaminated materials, treating affected surfaces, and improving ventilation. In Houston's humid climate, ongoing moisture control is essential to prevent odor recurrence.

Animal Hoarding Cleanup

Animal hoarding situations present unique challenges including heavy concentrations of urine, feces, hair, and dander throughout the home. These materials can saturate subflooring, drywall, and structural elements. Our team uses specialized enzyme treatments and biohazard protocols to address animal waste contamination.

We coordinate with animal control and rescue organizations when animals are still present. Our focus is on making the home safe and sanitary while ensuring the animals receive appropriate care.

Garage, Attic & Storage Cleanouts

Garages, attics, storage units, and sheds are common overflow areas for hoarding. These spaces often go unaddressed for years and can harbor pests, mold, and fire hazards. Houston's extreme heat makes attic cleanouts particularly challenging — temperatures can exceed 150°F in summer, creating dangerous working conditions that require professional crews with proper safety equipment.

Commercial Cleanouts

We provide commercial cleanout services for offices, warehouses, retail spaces, and rental properties. Property managers and landlords rely on us to handle tenant cleanouts, foreclosure cleanouts, and commercial space restoration. We work on your timeline and can coordinate with your team to minimize disruption to business operations.
