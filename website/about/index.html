Every investigator in our network has been vetted for current LSBPIE licensure, professional conduct, area expertise, and client satisfaction. We continuously monitor their standing and only maintain relationships with investigators who uphold the highest professional standards.

What is the LSBPIE?

The Louisiana State Board of Private Investigator Examiners (LSBPIE) is the state regulatory body that licenses and oversees all private investigators operating in Louisiana. Established under Louisiana Revised Statute 37:3501 et seq., the LSBPIE sets education, training, and conduct requirements for licensed PIs.

To obtain a Louisiana PI license, an applicant must have a minimum of three years' experience in investigative work (or equivalent education), pass a written examination, pass a background check, and maintain liability insurance. Licensed PIs must renew their licenses regularly and comply with the LSBPIE's code of conduct.

It is illegal to perform private investigation work for compensation in Louisiana without a valid LSBPIE license. You can verify any PI's license status at the LSBPIE's official website. We encourage all clients to verify the license of any PI they hire — including those we refer.
