Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about private investigation services in Metairie and Louisiana.

How much does a private investigator cost in Metairie?
PI rates in the Metairie and New Orleans area typically range from $85–$150 per hour for standard surveillance and investigation services. Complex cases involving multiple investigators, specialized equipment, or rush assignments may cost more. Contact us for a free, no-obligation estimate tailored to your situation.
How do I verify a PI is licensed in Louisiana?
All PIs must be licensed by the Louisiana State Board of Private Investigator Examiners (LSBPIE). You can verify a license online at the LSBPIE website. Every investigator we refer holds a current, valid Louisiana PI license.
Is surveillance legal in Louisiana?
Yes, when conducted by a licensed investigator following Louisiana law. Surveillance in public places is legal. PIs cannot trespass or install hidden recording devices in private spaces. A licensed PI understands these legal boundaries and ensures evidence is admissible.
How confidential is a private investigation?
Licensed PIs in Louisiana are bound by professional confidentiality standards. Your case details, identity, and findings are kept strictly private. We never share client information with third parties. All consultations are 100% confidential.
