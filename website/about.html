Our Story

Why We Do This Work

Hoarding Cleanup Houston was founded with a simple belief: everyone deserves to live in a safe, clean home — and everyone deserves to be treated with dignity when asking for help.

We understand that hoarding is not a choice. It is a recognized mental health condition that affects people from all walks of life. When families reach out to us, they are often at a vulnerable point. They may feel embarrassed, overwhelmed, or unsure where to start. Our job is to lift that burden.

Every member of our team is trained not just in cleanup techniques, but in compassionate communication. We never judge. We never rush. We work at a pace that respects the homeowner's emotional connection to their belongings while steadily restoring their home to a safe condition.

Based in Houston, we understand the unique challenges our climate presents — from rapid mold growth in humid conditions to pest issues that thrive in our warm environment. This local expertise allows us to address not just the visible clutter, but the hidden hazards that can make hoarding situations in Houston particularly dangerous.

Who We Help

We Work with Families, Professionals & Organizations

Our clients come from many different situations. We adapt our approach to meet each one's needs.

👨‍👩‍👧

Families & Loved Ones

Adult children helping aging parents, spouses supporting a partner, or families addressing a loved one's hoarding condition. We guide you through every step of the process with care and patience.

🏥

Social Workers & Therapists

Mental health professionals and case managers who need a reliable, compassionate cleanup partner for their clients. We coordinate with treatment teams and respect therapeutic boundaries.

🏢

Property Managers & Landlords

When tenants leave behind hoarding situations, property managers need fast, thorough cleanup to restore units. We handle the entire process, including biohazard remediation and deep cleaning.

⚖️

Estate Executors & Attorneys

Handling an inherited property that has been hoarded requires professional cleanup before the property can be sold or transferred. We provide documentation and work within legal timelines.
