Private Investigation Services in Metairie, LA

Licensed investigators in the Metairie area offer a full range of professional investigation services for individuals, attorneys, and businesses.

What Investigators Offer

Investigation Services Available in Metairie

From surveillance and background checks to digital forensics, licensed investigators in the Metairie area are equipped to handle a wide variety of cases.

Surveillance

A licensed investigator can conduct covert, stationary, and mobile surveillance to document subject activity and gather evidence. Surveillance operations are tailored to each case, whether monitoring a single location or following a subject across multiple areas. Investigators in Metairie use professional-grade equipment to capture clear, court-admissible documentation.

Background Checks & Due Diligence

Thorough background investigations help verify an individual's criminal history, employment records, financial standing, and personal references. Licensed investigators access databases and public records that are not readily available to the general public. These checks are commonly requested by employers, landlords, attorneys, and individuals entering business partnerships.

Skip Tracing & Locates

When someone cannot be found through conventional means, a licensed investigator uses specialized databases, public records, and field techniques to locate the individual. Skip tracing is frequently used to find missing persons, debtors, witnesses, or biological relatives. Investigators in the Metairie area have extensive experience locating individuals throughout Louisiana and beyond.

Process Serving

Professional process servers ensure that legal documents such as subpoenas, summonses, and court orders are delivered in accordance with Louisiana state law. Timely and proper service of process is critical to the legal system. Investigators handling process serving in Metairie maintain detailed records and provide affidavits of service for court filings.

Asset Investigations

Asset investigations uncover hidden bank accounts, real estate holdings, vehicle registrations, business interests, and other financial assets. These investigations are often requested in connection with divorce proceedings, litigation, or judgment collection. A licensed investigator can compile a comprehensive asset profile through database research and field work.

Cheating Spouse & Infidelity Investigations

Infidelity investigations are among the most commonly requested private investigation services. A licensed investigator can discreetly monitor a spouse's activities, document suspicious behavior, and provide clear photographic or video evidence. All findings are handled with strict confidentiality and can be presented in court if needed for divorce or custody proceedings.

Child Custody Investigations

In custody disputes, the wellbeing of the child is the top priority. A licensed investigator can document a parent's living conditions, daily routines, and any behaviors that may be relevant to a custody determination. The evidence gathered during these investigations is often pivotal in family court proceedings across Jefferson Parish and surrounding areas.

Insurance Fraud & Workers' Comp Surveillance

Insurance companies and employers rely on licensed investigators to verify the legitimacy of claims. Surveillance and background research can reveal inconsistencies between reported injuries and actual activities. Investigators in the Metairie area regularly work with insurance adjusters and legal teams to document fraudulent claims with court-admissible evidence.

Digital Investigations & OSINT

Open-source intelligence (OSINT) and digital investigations involve gathering information from publicly available online sources, social media platforms, and digital footprints. A licensed investigator can build detailed profiles, verify identities, and uncover connections that are not immediately visible. These techniques are increasingly valuable in fraud, missing persons, and litigation support cases.
