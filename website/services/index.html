Surveillance

Surveillance is the cornerstone of private investigation work in Louisiana. Our referred PIs conduct professional covert surveillance using state-of-the-art equipment to document activities, movements, and behaviors relevant to your case.

Whether you need evidence for a legal proceeding, want to verify someone's whereabouts, or need documentation of a pattern of behavior, our investigators deliver. All surveillance is conducted in compliance with Louisiana law — from public spaces and legal vantage points.

Mobile surveillance follows subjects across the Greater New Orleans area including Metairie, Kenner, Harahan, and neighboring parishes. Stationary surveillance covers fixed locations like residences, businesses, and meeting spots. All footage is time-stamped and legally documented for court use.

Our investigators carry the latest HD video cameras, long-range lenses, and GPS tracking equipment (where legally permitted). Reports are comprehensive, detailed, and formatted for use by attorneys and courts.

Most surveillance cases in the Metairie area are resolved within 3–7 days of engagement. Rush surveillance is available for time-sensitive matters. Call (504) 833-3716 for a free consultation.

Infidelity Investigation

Suspecting a cheating spouse or partner is one of the most emotionally difficult situations a person can face. Our referred private investigators handle infidelity cases with absolute discretion, professionalism, and sensitivity.

We understand the emotional weight of these investigations. Our PIs are trained to gather factual evidence efficiently without causing unnecessary distress. Evidence gathered is documented in a format suitable for use in divorce proceedings, custody disputes, and legal matters.

An infidelity investigation typically involves surveillance of the subject's activities, documentation of meetings, verification of stories and alibis, and digital intelligence gathering where legally permitted. Louisiana is a no-fault divorce state, but evidence of infidelity can still impact settlement and custody outcomes.

Our investigators will never make assumptions or present unverified information. Every finding is documented with timestamps, GPS data, photos, and/or video. Your investigation is completely confidential — we never contact the subject or reveal your identity.

Cases typically involve 2–5 days of surveillance depending on the subject's patterns. Contact us for a free, judgment-free consultation.

Child custody cases demand solid, factual evidence. Our referred investigators specialize in gathering documentation that protects children and supports your legal position in Jefferson Parish family court proceedings.

Background Checks

A thorough background investigation can protect you from fraud, deception, and dangerous individuals. Our referred investigators conduct comprehensive background checks for individuals, businesses, and organizations across Metairie and Louisiana.

Background investigations go far beyond what you can find online. Our PIs access court records, property records, employment history, professional license verifications, civil and criminal history, and much more. We compile this into a clear, comprehensive report.

Personal background checks are used before entering into romantic relationships, making new business partnerships, hiring contractors, or verifying someone you've met online. Business background checks help vet employees, partners, vendors, and investment opportunities.

Our investigators also specialize in verifying professional credentials, identifying undisclosed identities or aliases, uncovering hidden assets, and verifying business registration and legitimacy. All background investigations comply with the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) and Louisiana state law.

Turnaround time for standard background checks is 24–72 hours. Expedited service is available. Contact us to discuss your specific needs.

Skip Tracing

Skip tracing is the professional process of locating individuals who have moved or gone missing, whether intentionally or not. Our referred investigators are experts in skip tracing across Louisiana and nationally.

Skip tracing serves many purposes: locating a debtor who owes money, finding a witness needed for legal proceedings, reconnecting with a lost family member, or locating a subject for service of process. Our investigators use a combination of database searches, public records, field work, and social media intelligence.

Louisiana has rich public records that experienced skip tracers know how to leverage. Combined with proprietary database access and field verification techniques, our investigators can locate most individuals within days. We operate in Metairie, Jefferson Parish, the Greater New Orleans area, and can work cases statewide.

All skip tracing is conducted in compliance with Louisiana privacy laws and the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act. We do not provide location information that would be used to harass or harm individuals.

Results are typically delivered within 24–72 hours for cooperative cases. Complex cases involving intentional evasion may take longer. Contact us for a free consultation.

Insurance Fraud Investigation

Insurance fraud costs Louisiana businesses and consumers billions of dollars annually. Our referred investigators specialize in uncovering fraudulent claims, staged accidents, exaggerated injuries, and workers' compensation fraud across the Greater New Orleans area.

Insurance fraud investigations involve covert surveillance of claimants to document actual physical capabilities versus reported limitations, social media monitoring, witness interviews, background investigation of claimants, and coordination with insurance company investigators and attorneys.

Our PIs have experience working with insurance companies, defense attorneys, and employers in Jefferson Parish and across Louisiana. We deliver comprehensive investigative reports with video evidence, photographic documentation, and detailed written findings that stand up in court.

Workers' compensation fraud is a particular specialty. If an employee claims to be unable to work or perform certain activities, our investigators can document their actual daily activities and physical capabilities through professional surveillance.

All investigations comply with Louisiana insurance regulations and privacy laws. Findings are presented in a format compatible with legal proceedings and insurance claim adjustments. Contact us for a confidential consultation.

Corporate & Workplace Investigation
