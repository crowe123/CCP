When it matters who someone really is — before you hire them, partner with them, or welcome them into your life — an internet background check site is not enough. These consumer-facing services pull from limited public data aggregators, carry outdated information, and cannot verify what they report. A licensed private investigator in Metairie conducts a true investigative background check: one that uses professional databases, courthouse-level document retrieval, personal interviews, and trained analytical judgment to build a complete, verified picture of an individual.

Consumer Background Check Sites vs. a Licensed PI

Services like BeenVerified, Spokeo, Intelius, and similar platforms collect publicly available data in bulk, but they cannot distinguish between individuals with similar names, they rarely access sealed or expunged court records accurately, and they frequently miss civil judgments, out-of-state criminal matters, and records from jurisdictions that don’t digitize filings. They sell volume, not accuracy.

A licensed Louisiana private investigator operates under LSBPIE (Louisiana State Board of Private Investigator Examiners) licensing requirements and carries professional access to law enforcement-grade databases, including CLEAR, TLO, IRB Search, and PACER for federal records. More importantly, a PI can pick up the phone, appear at a courthouse, or conduct a reference interview — actions no algorithm can replicate.

Before engaging any investigator, it’s worth reading our guide on how to verify a PI’s license in Louisiana to ensure the person you hire is properly credentialed with the LSBPIE.

What a PI Background Check Can Include

Criminal History

A PI can retrieve criminal records at the parish, state, and federal level. In Louisiana, criminal history records are maintained by the Louisiana State Police Bureau of Criminal Identification and Information. For Jefferson Parish residents, the Jefferson Parish Sheriff’s Office (JPSO) maintains local records. A PI can access arrest records, conviction histories, pending charges, and in many cases sex offender registry entries. Federal criminal records, including those in the Eastern District of Louisiana headquartered in New Orleans, are accessible through PACER.

Civil Court Records

Louisiana’s civil court system operates across district courts, city courts, and federal courts. Jefferson Parish’s 24th Judicial District Court handles civil litigation including lawsuits, judgments, and restraining orders. A PI can retrieve judgments against a subject, past or active civil litigation, bankruptcies, liens, and foreclosure actions — all of which speak to a person’s reliability and financial behavior.

Employment Verification

A PI can contact past employers directly to verify dates of employment, title, and — when legally available — circumstances of departure. This goes beyond what automated employment verification services report, particularly for individuals who may have misrepresented their work history on applications or profiles.

Education Verification

Degree fraud is more common than most people assume. A PI can contact universities and trade schools directly to verify that claimed degrees, certifications, and attendance records are accurate. This is especially relevant when vetting professionals claiming advanced credentials.

Address History and Alias Identification

A professional database search reveals where a subject has actually lived, the names they have used, and any known aliases or maiden names. This is foundational to understanding whether other records retrieved are complete. For more on how PIs use these databases, see our article on what a PI can find through OSINT and open-source research.

Professional License Verification

Louisiana maintains professional licensing databases through the Louisiana Department of Health, the Louisiana State Bar Association, the Louisiana Real Estate Commission, and dozens of other licensing bodies. A PI can verify that a subject holds the licenses they claim and that those licenses are in good standing without disciplinary action.

Financial and Asset Information

While a PI cannot pull a credit report without the subject’s consent (that is governed by the Fair Credit Reporting Act), an investigator can legally research publicly recorded assets including real property, vehicle registrations, watercraft, and UCC filings. This asset picture is valuable when entering business partnerships, considering a loan, or conducting due diligence on a potential spouse. Read our article on skip tracing in Metairie for more on how PIs locate and document assets.

Reference Interviews

For high-stakes background investigations, a PI can conduct personal interviews with former colleagues, neighbors, landlords, or professional references. These conversations often reveal character information, work ethic, disputes, and behavioral patterns that no database reflects.

When Do You Need a PI Background Check?

Hiring a Contractor or Home Service Professional

Bringing someone into your home in Metairie or elsewhere in Jefferson Parish carries real risk. A licensed contractor background check by a PI verifies not just criminal history, but also whether the individual holds a valid Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors license, has active civil judgments from prior clients, or has operated under multiple business names after complaints.

Romantic Relationships and New Partners

When a new relationship moves quickly — especially one involving finances, children, or cohabitation — a discrete background check can surface prior marriages, restraining orders, criminal history, or inconsistencies in the person’s stated background. Our article on what to expect during a marital investigation covers related concerns for those already in a relationship.

Business Partner Due Diligence

Before signing a partnership agreement, investing capital, or joining an LLC with another party, a PI background check can reveal prior business failures, fraud allegations, civil judgments, and patterns of financial misconduct. This is due diligence in the truest sense.

Landlords in Metairie and Jefferson Parish can commission a PI background check to verify a prospective tenant’s rental history, eviction record (accessible through Louisiana civil courts), criminal background, and employment claims — going far deeper than any rental application service.

Nanny, Caregiver, and Household Employee Screening

Before trusting someone with children, elderly parents, or an individual with special needs, a comprehensive PI background check is invaluable. Unlike consumer services, a PI can verify that an individual’s professional certifications are legitimate, contact prior families, and surface any records related to abuse, neglect, or theft — issues that may not appear in a standard criminal history if charges were reduced or dismissed.

What Information Should You Provide?

To conduct an effective background check, your PI will typically need: the subject’s full legal name and any known aliases, date of birth, Social Security number (if available and legally provided), known addresses (current and historical), and the specific scope of information you need. The more context you provide, the faster and more accurate the investigation. A skilled PI can work from less information, but depth of data directly affects thoroughness.

Cost and Turnaround

PI background check fees in the Metairie and greater New Orleans market typically range from $200 for a basic criminal history and address verification to $2,000 or more for a comprehensive due diligence investigation that includes reference interviews, asset research, and multi-state court record retrieval. Turnaround time for a basic report is commonly 24–72 hours; comprehensive investigations may require one to two weeks. Legal Compliance: FCRA and PI Reports

It is important to understand that the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) applies specifically to consumer reports prepared by consumer reporting agencies (CRAs) for employment, credit, housing, or insurance decisions. A private investigative background check conducted for personal knowledge, civil litigation support, or private due diligence does not automatically constitute a consumer report under the FCRA. However, if a PI report is used by an employer to make an adverse hiring decision, FCRA obligations may apply, and you should consult with legal counsel before using report findings in that context. Louisiana law adds additional layers through the Louisiana Consumer Credit Law, and compliance requirements vary by intended use.
