## Hourly Rates: What to Expect in the New Orleans Metro



Licensed private investigators in the Metairie and New Orleans area typically bill between **$85 and $150 per hour** for standard surveillance and field work. This range reflects the mid-size city market — rates are lower than major metros like New York or Los Angeles, but higher than rural Louisiana markets where competition is thinner and caseloads smaller.



Within that range, expect to pay toward the higher end for:




- Complex surveillance requiring two investigators working simultaneously


- Cases involving specialized equipment such as long-range cameras or night-vision gear


- Investigations that cross parish lines frequently, adding drive time


- Highly experienced investigators with law enforcement or military backgrounds


 

A solo investigator handling a routine [infidelity surveillance](../articles/cheating-spouse-investigation-what-to-expect.html) in a residential area of Metairie or River Ridge will typically fall in the $85–$110 range. More involved cases command higher rates.



## Retainer Amounts: The Deposit That Starts Your Case



Most licensed Louisiana PIs do not work on contingency — they require an upfront retainer before any investigative work begins. In the New Orleans metro, typical retainers range from **$1,500 to $3,000**, though the amount depends on the anticipated scope of the case.



The retainer functions as a prepaid deposit held against the investigator's hourly rate. As hours are worked and expenses are incurred, costs are drawn from that balance. A reputable agency will provide itemized billing at regular intervals showing exactly how your retainer was applied. If your case is resolved before the retainer is exhausted, most investigators will refund the unused balance — confirm this policy in writing before signing anything.



If a case exceeds the initial retainer — for instance, a [child custody investigation in Jefferson Parish](../articles/child-custody-investigation-jefferson-parish.html) that extends over multiple weeks — you will be asked to replenish the retainer. Build this contingency into your budget from the start.

## Total Investigation Costs by Case Type



Understanding the total likely cost — not just the hourly rate — helps you plan realistically.



### Infidelity Investigations



A [cheating spouse investigation](../articles/cheating-spouse-investigation-what-to-expect.html) in Metairie typically involves two to four surveillance sessions of four to six hours each. At $90–$120 per hour, with a two-investigator team for some sessions, total costs generally fall between **$1,250 and $4,500**. The lower end applies to short, conclusive cases; the upper end reflects longer surveillance periods where the subject's patterns are unpredictable.



### Child Custody Investigations



Custody-related investigations are among the most extensive PI engagements. Documenting a parent's living conditions, daily routines, and the people their children are exposed to may require multiple surveillance days spread over several weeks. Total costs for a [child custody investigation in Jefferson Parish](../articles/child-custody-investigation-jefferson-parish.html) typically range from **$3,000 to $8,000**, and can exceed that figure in contested cases involving extended monitoring.



### Insurance Fraud Investigations



When an insurance company, employer, or attorney engages a PI to document a potentially fraudulent claim, costs typically run **$1,500 to $5,000** for a standard surveillance operation. Read more about [insurance fraud investigations in Louisiana](../articles/insurance-fraud-investigation-louisiana.html) and how evidence is documented for use in court.



## Factors That Affect the Total Cost of Your Investigation


### Case Complexity



A subject who works a predictable 9-to-5 schedule and lives in a residential neighborhood is straightforward to surveil. A subject who works irregular hours, travels frequently between New Orleans parishes, or is actively evasive requires more investigative time and often more personnel.



### Team Size



Some surveillance scenarios require two investigators — one to follow on foot, one to maintain vehicle surveillance — to avoid detection. Team rates are typically billed at 1.5x to 2x the single-investigator rate.



### Equipment and Technology



High-quality covert cameras, long-range lenses, and video stabilization equipment may be billed as a separate daily equipment fee ranging from $50 to $150 per day, or factored into a higher hourly rate. Always ask how equipment costs are handled.



### Mileage and Expense Billing



Most PIs in Louisiana bill mileage at the current IRS rate (67 cents per mile as of 2024) for travel beyond a defined radius. Parking fees, tolls, and incidental expenses are typically passed through at cost. A case that takes an investigator from Metairie to Mandeville across Lake Pontchartrain will include bridge tolls and extended drive time.

## Why the Cheapest Investigator Often Costs You More



An unlicensed investigator or one who cuts corners on surveillance methodology can produce evidence that is inadmissible in a Louisiana court — or worse, evidence that was gathered illegally, exposing you to civil or criminal liability. Louisiana courts have consistently excluded evidence obtained through trespass, illegal recording, or unlawful GPS tracking under R.S. 14:323. Paying $80 an hour for improperly gathered evidence that gets thrown out of a custody hearing can cost you far more in attorney fees and lost case outcomes than a properly conducted $120-per-hour investigation would have.



Licensing matters. The Louisiana State Board of Private Investigator Examiners requires licensed investigators to meet training standards, carry proper insurance, and adhere to state law. Learn more about [how to verify a PI's Louisiana license](../articles/verify-pi-license-louisiana.html) before you commit to any engagement.



## Budgeting Realistically for Your Investigation



The most common mistake clients make is underbudgeting and then terminating an investigation prematurely before actionable evidence is obtained. Here is a simple framework for setting your budget:




- Estimate the number of surveillance sessions you expect to need — typically three to five for a focused case.


- Multiply the expected hours per session (four to eight) by the investigator's hourly rate.


- Add 20% as a contingency for unexpected case developments.


- Factor in flat fees for any supplemental services such as a background check or database search.


 

When you call for a consultation, a qualified PI will give you an honest estimate based on the specifics of your situation. That conversation is free and confidential — there is no obligation, and the information you share is protected by client confidentiality throughout the engagement.
