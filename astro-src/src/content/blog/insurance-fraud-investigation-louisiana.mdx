## The Scale of Insurance Fraud in Louisiana



The Louisiana Department of Insurance's Office of Insurance Fraud is a dedicated investigative unit that accepts referrals from insurers, healthcare providers, and the public. Louisiana's geographic density in the New Orleans metro area — combined with high rates of litigation and a plaintiff-friendly legal culture — makes the state a frequent target for organized fraud rings as well as opportunistic individual claimants.



Insurance fraud takes money directly from Louisiana businesses and families through higher premiums. Workers' compensation fraud in particular is a documented problem in industries like construction, hospitality, and maritime work that are prevalent in the Jefferson Parish economy. When an employer or insurer engages a licensed PI, they are taking a direct step to protect those resources.



## Types of Insurance Fraud Investigations



### Workers' Compensation Fraud



This is the most common type of insurance fraud investigation conducted by PIs in Louisiana. A claimant receiving workers' compensation benefits for a workplace injury may be exaggerating their disability — or working a second job while claiming they cannot perform any physical activity. Covert video surveillance of a claimant performing activities inconsistent with their stated limitations is the primary investigative tool, and the results can be compelling evidence in claim disputes and litigation.



### Personal Injury Fraud



Personal injury claims arising from auto accidents on I-10 through Metairie, slip-and-fall incidents at Jefferson Parish businesses, or premises liability claims sometimes involve exaggerated or fabricated injuries. A PI documenting a claimant lifting heavy objects, exercising, or performing activities contradicting their claimed injury provides the defense attorney or insurance adjuster with direct visual evidence.



### Auto Insurance Fraud



Staged accidents, inflated repair claims, and medical billing schemes are documented problems in the greater New Orleans area. PIs may assist by conducting [background checks in Metairie](../articles/background-check-metairie.html) on claimants to identify prior fraud patterns, locating witnesses, and conducting surveillance to document post-accident physical capabilities.

## Methods Used in Insurance Fraud Investigations



### Covert Video Surveillance



The cornerstone of any physical injury fraud investigation is covert video documentation of the claimant's actual physical capabilities. A licensed PI will observe the subject in public spaces — the parking lot of a grocery store in Metairie, a park in Jefferson Parish, a worksite — and document activities that contradict their claimed limitations. All surveillance is conducted from lawful public locations, without trespass and without violating the subject's reasonable expectation of privacy in their home. Read about [surveillance laws in Louisiana](../articles/surveillance-laws-louisiana-pi.html) for a full explanation of what is permissible.



### Activity Documentation and Reporting



A professional fraud investigation produces more than raw video footage. The PI prepares a detailed written report documenting every observation: dates, times, locations, subject identification, activities observed, and the chain of custody for all video and photographic evidence. This documentation is structured for use by claims adjusters, defense attorneys, and — if litigation proceeds — as evidence in the 24th Judicial District Court or other Louisiana courts.



### Social Media Monitoring



Claimants who are privately careful about their activities are often careless online. A person claiming total disability who posts photos from a golf course, a gym, or a construction job site creates their own evidentiary problem. Licensed PIs conduct [open source intelligence (OSINT) investigations](../articles/osint-what-pi-can-find-online.html) of publicly available social media profiles and online activity as a lawful and often highly productive investigative tool.



### Witness Interviews and Background Research



Interviews with neighbors, coworkers, or business associates who have observed the claimant's actual physical capabilities can provide corroborating testimony. Background research may reveal prior fraudulent claims, prior arrests for fraud, or inconsistencies in the claimant's personal and financial history. A comprehensive [background check](../articles/background-check-metairie.html) conducted by a licensed PI can surface this information in a form usable by attorneys and adjusters.



## Legal Authority: What a PI Can and Cannot Do



It is important to understand that licensed private investigators in Louisiana are not law enforcement. They cannot make arrests, compel testimony, or access records that require a court order or law enforcement authority. However, within the substantial investigative toolkit available to them — public records, lawful surveillance, database searches, interviews, and OSINT — experienced PIs can build comprehensive evidentiary files that give attorneys and insurance companies a clear picture of actual claimant behavior.



A PI investigating insurance fraud must comply with all of the same [surveillance laws that govern any Louisiana private investigation](../articles/surveillance-laws-louisiana-pi.html). No trespass, no unlawful recording, no impersonation of law enforcement, no non-consensual GPS tracking under R.S. 14:323. Evidence gathered in violation of these rules is inadmissible and may expose the insurer or employer to civil liability.

## Admissibility of PI Evidence in Louisiana Courts



Properly gathered surveillance video, photographs, and investigative reports are admissible in Louisiana civil and criminal proceedings when the evidence was lawfully obtained and properly documented. The chain of custody — demonstrating that the evidence was not altered or tampered with between collection and presentation — is critical and must be maintained throughout the investigation.



Defense attorneys working fraud cases regularly present PI surveillance video as exhibit evidence in depositions, hearings, and trials. Louisiana courts have recognized the probative value of properly documented PI evidence in personal injury and workers' compensation disputes. For PI evidence to reach this stage effectively, the investigator must be a licensed professional who can be qualified as a witness if necessary. Always [verify a PI's Louisiana license](../articles/verify-pi-license-louisiana.html) before engaging them for any investigation that may produce courtroom evidence.

## Working with Insurance Adjusters and Attorneys



The most effective insurance fraud investigations are coordinated from the outset between the insurance adjuster or claims manager, the defense attorney, and the PI. The attorney sets the evidentiary parameters — what needs to be documented, what standards will apply, and how results will be used. The adjuster provides the factual background on the claim. The PI designs the investigative plan to gather what is needed within those legal boundaries.
